Questions

Frequently asked

Who can apply?

Women aged 18–28 for Miss, married women aged 25–45 for Mrs. and girls aged 14–17 for Teen, from any country where we have a national director.

Do I need pageant experience?

No. The Queen Academy prepares every delegate for the stage, the jury and the media.

What happens after pre-registration?

Your national director reviews your profile and invites you to the national casting in your country.

Are there any fees?

Participation terms vary by country and are shared by your national director before the casting.

How are results announced?

Results are announced on coronation night and published on this site and our official social media accounts.

01About

A new era of queenship

Miss Queen Era World is an international organisation that sees beauty as a starting point — and defines the true crown as something earned through character, vision and contribution to society.

Each season, delegates selected from across the globe are trained in protocol, public speaking, stagecraft and social impact — becoming cultural ambassadors for their nations. Because we don’t just choose queens. We raise them.

— The Founding Board

02Virtues

Six virtues of a queen

Our jury evaluates the person, not just the runway. Every delegate is scored on these six principles.

I

Elegance

Grace in posture, word and manner — on stage and in life.

II

Intelligence

A mind that reads the world, asks questions and defends ideas with courage.

III

Confidence

The strength to be wholly yourself — and to inspire others to do the same.

IV

Culture

An identity rooted in heritage, building bridges to other cultures.

V

Compassion

Wearing the crown not as a privilege, but as a responsibility.

VI

Leadership

The vision and resolve to move a community forward.

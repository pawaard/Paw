Goodwill Ambassadors

A crown is a promise

Throughout her reign, every queen serves as a Goodwill Ambassador, leading projects for girls’ education, children’s health and the preservation of cultural heritage.

01Our causes

Three causes, one heart

I

Girls’ Education

Scholarships, mentoring and school supplies for girls in the communities our queens visit.

II

Children’s Health

Hospital visits, awareness campaigns and fundraising with partner health organisations.

III

Cultural Heritage

Projects that protect crafts, music and traditions — and carry them to new generations.

02How it works

From a cause to change

  1. 01

    Choose a cause

    Every queen picks a cause close to her heart at the start of her reign.

  2. 02

    Build the project

    With our team and partners she designs a project with clear, measurable goals.

  3. 03

    Share the impact

    Progress is reported throughout the year and celebrated at the next Grand Final.
